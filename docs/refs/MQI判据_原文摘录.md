## 编码 3. Explanations（Richness of the Mathematics 域）

**原文（定义）**：

> Mathematical explanations focus on **why**, e.g.:
> - Why a procedure works (or doesn't work)
> - Why a solution method is appropriate (or inappropriate)
> - Why an answer is true (or not true)
> - In geometry: justification using a definition, why an object is symmetrical, why a second figure is a transformation of the first
> - In data analysis: why you would choose a specific graph to represent a set of data, why median is different than mode or mean of a dataset, etc.
>
> **Do NOT count "how"** e.g., simply providing descriptions of steps (first I did x, then I did y) or definitions unless meaning is also attached.
>
> **Note: Do NOT count incorrect or incomplete explanations as explanations.**

**四级（原文）**：

| 级 | 原文 |
|---|---|
| Not Present | No mathematical explanations are offered by the teacher or students or the "explanations" provided are simply descriptions of steps of a procedure. |
| Low | A mathematical explanation occurs as an **isolated instance** in the segment. |
| Mid | **Two or more brief** mathematical explanations occur in the segment **OR** an explanation is **more than briefly present but not the focus** of instruction. |
| High | One or more mathematical explanation(s) is **a focus of instruction** in the segment. The explanation(s) need not be most or even a majority of the segment; what distinguishes a High is the fact that the explanation(s) are a **major feature** of the teacher-student work. |

**评分帮助（原文）**：

> Note that when scoring, you can count the **build-up to an explanation as part of the explanation**.
> Ask yourself: Was the point of the instruction to provide the explanation, even if it only emerged at the end?
> If so, you may score that clip as a High.

**极性**：**高分是好的**（Richness 域）。

## 编码 4. Mathematical Sense-Making（Richness of the Mathematics 域）

**原文（定义）**：捕捉教师或学生是否关注以下之一：

> - The meaning of numbers
> - Understanding relationships between numbers
> - The relationships between contexts and the numbers or operations that represent them
> - Connections between mathematical ideas or between ideas and representations
> - Giving meaning to mathematical ideas
> - Whether the modeling of and answers to problems make sense

**例子（原文）**：关注量的值（"7/8 is close to 1"）、量的含义、**讨论表达式/解法/答案的合理性**、
估算与数感、**给程序赋予意义**、给表达式或方程赋予意义。
几何里包括**让定义与公式有意义**（细化、应用、**找反例**），而不是只陈述或执行。

**四级（原文）**：

| 级 | 原文 |
|---|---|
| Not Present | Not present or incorrect. |
| Low | Teacher and/or students focus **briefly** on meaning. |
| Mid | focus on meaning **more than briefly** (several instances within the segment or one somewhat long instance), but this work is **not sustained or substantial**. |
| High | focus on meaning in a **sustained** way during segment. Need not be the entire segment, but must be **substantial**. |

**与 Explanations 的关键区别（原文）**：

> - a High for Explanations means that the instance is **the main feature** of the segment,
>   whereas under Sense-Making it just needs to be **sustained**.
> - when scoring Explanations, **you can count the build-up** as part of the explanation…;
>   when scoring Sense-Making, **only count time in which sense is actually being made**;
>   we do **not** count related procedural work or build-up as sense-making.

**极性**：**高分是好的**。

## 编码 12. Mathematical Content Errors（Errors and Imprecision 域）

**原文（定义）**：捕捉数学上**不正确**的事件。例如：解题错误、**把术语定义错**、
**定义里漏掉关键条件**、**把两个不相同的数学概念等同**。
学生犯的错若被教师认可，也要记；教师把正确解法判为错误也要记。
**不计**：故意的错误、**在段内被纠正的错误**。

**四级（原文）**：

| 级 | 原文 |
|---|---|
| Not Present | None. |
| Low | A brief content error. **Does not obscure the mathematics** of the segment. |
| Mid | Content errors occur in **part(s)** of the segment. **OR** Error(s) **obscure the mathematics, but for only part** of the segment. |
| High | Content errors occur in **most or all** of the segment. **OR** The errors **obscure the mathematics** of the segment. |

**极性**：⚠️ **高分是坏的**（Errors and Imprecision 域）。**混用会算反——这是已知的极性陷阱。**

## 编码 13. Imprecision in Language or Notation（Errors and Imprecision 域）

**原文（定义）**：捕捉数学语言或记号的问题。三类：

> - **Notation**：常规数学符号（+, -, = 等）的误用（等号、括号、除号等）
> - **Mathematical language**：技术术语（angle / equation / perimeter 等）用错
> - **General language**：用非技术语言讲数学概念时的含混；也包括类比、隐喻、故事。
>   **"Appropriate use of terms includes care in distinguishing everyday meanings different from their mathematical meanings."**
>   若在一般表述中对数学概念讲得不清楚 → 记为错误。

**四级**：Not Present=None；Low=简短一处、不影响理解；Mid=出现在部分；High=出现在大部分/影响理解。
（原文表格结构与编码 12 相同。）

**极性**：⚠️ **高分是坏的**。

## 编码 14. Lack of Clarity in Presentation of Mathematical Content（Errors and Imprecision 域）

**原文（定义）**：捕捉教师的话**无法被理解**的情况。例如：数学要点**含混、令人困惑或被歪曲**；
语言或重大错误使人难以看出要点；**没有把问题讲清楚**。

**四级**：Not Present=None；Low=简短一处、不影响理解；Mid=出现在部分；High=出现在大部分/影响理解。

**评分帮助（原文）**：问自己 **"What, mathematically, was the teacher trying to say?"**
给出的例子之一是：**教师对同一概念给出互相冲突的定义**。

**极性**：⚠️ **高分是坏的**。

## 本项目使用时的**映射说明**（必须随结论一起给出）

MQI 是**整段课堂观察**量表（一个 segment ≈ 5–7.5 分钟），分级依据是**时间与篇幅**
（"isolated instance in the segment"、"occurs in part(s) of the segment"）。
我们的对象是**一份静态讲解文本**，没有"段"这个概念。

| 原文措辞 | 本项目的映射 |
|---|---|
| "in the segment" | **整篇讲解**（把整篇当一个 segment） |
| "isolated instance" | 全篇只出现 **1 处** |
| "two or more brief" / "more than briefly" | 出现 **≥2 处**或**有 1 处篇幅较长** |
| "focus of instruction" / "major feature" | **是这篇讲解的主要特征** |
| "sustained"（编码 4） | 该行为**贯穿相当篇幅** |

> ⚠️ **这个映射是我做的，没有经过验证。** 所以结论只能说
> "**按上述映射**，在这批样本上……"，**不能**说"MQI 评分显示……"。
