This quarter covered a literature and design stage, then a first working prototype.

- **Literature and design.** I reviewed work on personalised learning and learner modelling (Shemshack & Spector, 2020; Tetzlaff et al., 2021), the ICAP framework (Chi & Wylie, 2014), meta-analyses and reviews of intelligent tutoring systems (Kulik & Fletcher, 2016; Zerkouk et al., 2025), and hybrid human-AI teaching (Cukurova, 2024; Molenaar). These corrected three assumptions behind my earlier plan: personalisation should be data-driven rather than student-selected, static grouping is insufficient, and a study without a reliable measure cannot proceed. They also exposed a gap to work around: evaluation in this field is inconsistent, and many systems are judged by self-made tests. The stage produced a student-profile design — three achievement emotions after Pekrun, an Active/Passive split of ICAP because the platform has no free-text input, and diagnostic classification modelling as the foundation — plus a staged plan and an open-question list for discussion.

- **Prototype system.** A Python/Flask application that retrieves courseware pages and generates explanations with DeepSeek, running two versions side by side for blind comparison, where only the prompts differ, so a difference is attributable to the prompt rather than to retrieval or the model. Chapter 1 of Rosen's Discrete Mathematics serves as the test dataset.

- **Evaluation.** Rather than writing my own rubric, I adopted the Mathematical Quality of Instruction scheme. Four rounds of blind scoring give the current version 2.45 against a 2.30 baseline. In a cold-start comparison, using identical courseware and questions, it scores 2.65 against 1.25 for a bare model.

- **Knowledge structure.** 114 nodes, 207 subgoal labels and 268 misconceptions for Chapter 1, with every quoted step verified against the courseware by script.

- **Outstanding work.** The profile remains a design: the platform logs only solving and explaining, and whether event order — needed to detect passive behaviour — is recorded is unconfirmed.
