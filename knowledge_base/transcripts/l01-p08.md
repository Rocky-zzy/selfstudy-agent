**1. Affine mapping**

Given two vector spaces $V, W$, a linear mapping $\phi: V \to W$ and $\vec{a} \in W$, the mapping $\phi': V \to W$ with

$$ \phi'(\vec{x}) = \vec{a} + \phi(\vec{x}) \quad \text{translation vector} $$

is called an affine mapping from $V$ to $W$
