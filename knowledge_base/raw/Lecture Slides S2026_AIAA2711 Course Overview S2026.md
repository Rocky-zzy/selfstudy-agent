•

•
When will it fail?
Why are the mathematical foundations of AI important?
•
Mathematics provides an rigorious framework for answering the questions above
•

•
To learn about the inherent assumptions and limitations of existing methodologies we are working with

Course AI Policy
Three-always principles for using (generative) AI in the course:
•
Always Acknowledge AI Contributions: When AI tools are deployed in coursework, it is 
essential to always cite and acknowledge their contributions properly. Transparency in the use of 
AI not only upholds academic integrity but also provides clarity on the sources of information and 
insights.
•
Always Critically Evaluate AI-Generated Information: AI tools can be powerful resources, but 
their outputs must be always assessed for accuracy and relevance critically. It is important to 
verify the information generated by AI against credible sources and apply independent judgment 
to ensure its validity and reliability in the coursework.
•
Always Prioritize Academic Integrity: AI should always only serve as a supplementary tool in 
the learning and research process, but not replacing personal efforts, understanding, and 
analysis in the coursework.
